## free-exercise-db

The exercise catalog in `Forge/Resources/exercises.json` is built from
[free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db) by yuhonas using `scripts/build_catalog.py`.
That dataset is released into the public domain under the [Unlicense](https://unlicense.org).
Exercise names for popular lifts were edited, and about 25 exercises with original instructions were added.
